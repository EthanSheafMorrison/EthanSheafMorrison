## Overview

Iterate was the 2024 postgraduate design showcase for Te Kura Hoahoa, the School of Design Innovation at Te Herenga Waka — Victoria University of Wellington. My role was to develop the exhibition's complete visual identity: a logo and mark, a typographic system, a grid-driven imagery language, and the print, motion, and spatial applications that carried it across the exhibition and its promotion.

The brief was to create a system flexible enough to hold together a cohort of wildly different research practices, yet distinctive enough to read as one show. The answer was *iteration* itself — a modular grid and a small set of repeatable elements that could be recombined endlessly, so every application felt like another pass at the same idea.

## Brand Identity

The wordmark stacks a solid *iterate* over a half-cut echo of itself — the logotype literally repeating and shifting, an iteration in the letterforms. Three red dots and a rising arrow act as supporting marks, drawn straight from the grid.

## Colour

The palette is deliberately spare: a single vermilion red against black and off-white paper. Red is reserved for accents, marks, and moments of emphasis; black carries the type; the grid does the rest. The restraint keeps 30-plus distinct research projects from competing, and makes the red read instantly as *Iterate*.

<Figure src="/images/projects/iterate/iterate-social-05.png" alt="Iterate square card showing the red, black and white palette on the grid" caption="One accent red, black type, white ground — the whole system in a single card." />

## Typeface

Type is limited to **Neue Haas Grotesk Display Pro** — Bold for headers, Medium for body, Italic for captions — set primarily in lower case to stay inline with the logotype. The Swiss neo-grotesk lineage anchors the whole system: tight, neutral, and built for the grid.

<Figure src="/images/projects/iterate/iterate-poster-13.png" alt="Iterate poster demonstrating the Neue Haas Grotesk type system on the grid" caption="Neue Haas Grotesk, set lower case and locked to the grid." />

## Imagery & Grid

Every layout is built on the same modular grid, and the imagery grows out of it — abstract line work, concentric curves, and halftone spheres that read as *iterations* of a single form. The studies below pan across that system: grid, mark, and abstract figure recombining as the eye moves.
