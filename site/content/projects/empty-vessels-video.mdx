### Overview
Exploring the shifting political grounds of global food production, <strong>Empty Vessels</strong> and <strong>Desert Narratives</strong> is a dialogue between Aotearoa New Zealand artist Matthew Galloway and Sahrawi Western Saharan artist Mohamed Sleiman Labat. The two present different perspectives on Aotearoa's reliance on phosphate rock from Western Sahara.

### Approach
I created video edits for the Circa Art Prize and supporting graphic assets contextualising the work’s broader kaupapa.
