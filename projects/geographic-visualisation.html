Overview

Geographic Visualisation is a design project exploring the intersection of spatial data, identity, and visual language. The work investigates how geographic information can be translated into compelling visual systems that communicate place, scale, and relationship beyond conventional cartographic conventions.

Approach

The identity system was built around the idea of modular rhythm—how distinct visual units could combine, repeat, and adapt across spatial and digital applications. The work explores how geographic data can be presented within a cohesive yet flexible system, allowing individual narratives and spatial relationships to stand out.
