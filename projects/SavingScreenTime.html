OVERVIEW

Saving Screen Time is a research and design project exploring the relationship between digital consumption, attention, and wellbeing. The project investigates how screen time habits shape daily life and how design interventions can foster more intentional engagement with technology.

APPROACH

The project combines behavioural research with speculative design to examine patterns of digital use. By documenting and analysing personal and collective screen time data, the work surfaces tensions between productivity, leisure, and digital dependency.

PROCESS

  • Research into digital wellbeing and attention economy literature.
  • Collection and analysis of personal screen time data.
  • Development of design responses and interventions.
  • Iteration through prototyping and testing.
