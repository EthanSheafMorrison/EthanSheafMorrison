OVERVIEW

Add Extra is a design and research project exploring themes of excess, supplementation, and the culture of addition. The project examines how layers of meaning, material, and context are accumulated in contemporary design practice.

APPROACH

The project takes an experimental approach, building on existing frameworks and systems to ask what happens when more is added. It interrogates notions of sufficiency, surplus, and the value of the supplementary.

PROCESS

  • Research into theories of surplus and supplementarity in design.
  • Development of design responses and experimental prototypes.
  • Testing and iterating on visual and material outcomes.
  • Documentation and reflection on the process of addition.
