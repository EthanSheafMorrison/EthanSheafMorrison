# Ethan Sheaf-Morrison

**Communication Designer, Researcher, and Educator**

I am a Wellington-based designer and researcher with a focus on critical cartography, data visualisation, and the representation of complex socio-environmental systems. Holding an MDes with Distinction from Te Herenga Waka, Victoria University of Wellington, my practice prioritises open-source methodologies, ethical hardware, and precise, modular logic. 

## Current Research & Projects

* **Embodied Cartographies:** Developing a digital archive and research platform (hosted on Vercel) that collates ongoing research into critical cartography and human/earth systems.
* **Saving Screen Time:** Serving as lead designer and communication lead for a Marsden Fast Start research project investigating smartphone use and internet experiences.
* **The Dream Atlas:** Collaborated as researcher and designer mapping historical dream archetypes, recently showcased at the MIT Dream Engineering Symposium.

## Technical & Methodological Focus

* **Aesthetics:** Grounded in functional, minimalist principles with a strong emphasis on clean typography and structured data representation.
* **Languages:** Fluent in English, proficient in German, and currently acquiring Norwegian and Te Reo Māori.

## Collaboration

I am actively engaged in academic collaborations and practice-based research across Europe and New Zealand. Feel free to explore my repositories or reach out regarding open-source design projects, spatial mapping, or data visualisation initiatives.
